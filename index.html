SIH26055DRDOElectronic Support

Problem in brief

An Electronic Support receiver sees only a narrow instantaneous bandwidth of a much wider spectrum, so it must decide where and when to look. Open-loop sweeps waste time on inactive regions and miss intermittent, periodic, agile or newly appearing emitters. This schema documents a closed-loop adaptive scheduler (PPO is one interchangeable decision model) that learns from HIT/MISS feedback to minimise interception time. Raw PDW fields (ToA, centre frequency, pulse width, AoA, amplitude — as in the Turing Synthetic Radar Dataset) are kept strictly separate from derived Smart Scan fields. The design is passive observation and scheduling research only.

Future Extensibility

Real SDR observations (source_type), multiple / simultaneous receivers (receiver_id on every scan), other schedulers (SCHEDULER_MODELS.model_type), online model updates (versioned models, ML_TRAINING_SAMPLES), larger datasets and dashboards (time-indexed facts, SYSTEM_EVENTS) and hardware-in-the-loop runs are supported by the existing keys — no schema change required. No weapon, targeting, jamming or transmission-control entities exist by design.
